Daily Inspo ("Daily Inspo," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Daily Inspo may collect, how that information is used and stored, and the choices available to you when you use our website, web application, and related services.

By using Daily Inspo, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Daily Inspo.

Account Information

If you create an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Your email address
  • Your account identifier
  • Authentication and account-related information necessary to provide your account

Daily Inspo uses third-party infrastructure, including Supabase, to provide services such as authentication, database storage, and cloud synchronization.

Quotes and Other Content

Daily Inspo allows users to create, save, organize, and manage quotes and related content. Depending on how you use the service, this content may be stored:

  • Locally on your browser or device
  • In Daily Inspo's cloud database when cloud synchronization is available or enabled
  • Publicly if you intentionally choose to make content public

Content marked private is intended to remain private to your account or device, subject to the technical operation of the service. If you choose to make content public, that content may be visible to other users and visitors.

Feedback and Support Information

If you send feedback, report a bug, request a feature, or contact Daily Inspo, we may collect:

  • The message you submit
  • The feedback category
  • Your email address, if provided
  • Your user ID, if you are signed in
  • The date and time of the submission

Feedback may be stored in our database and may also generate an email notification so that we can review and respond to it.

Local and Offline Data

Daily Inspo is designed with offline usability in mind. Some information, including quotes, application state, preferences, onboarding state, and cached content, may be stored locally in your browser or on your device.

Local data generally remains on that browser or device unless it is synchronized with an account or cloud service. Clearing browser storage, removing site data, changing browsers, or changing devices may remove locally stored information that has not been synchronized.

Technical Information

Our hosting providers and other infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser or device type
  • Request information
  • Error and security logs
  • Approximate timestamps of service activity

Daily Inspo does not currently intend to collect more technical information than is reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate Daily Inspo
  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Save and synchronize user content
  • Support offline and cross-device functionality
  • Display content that users intentionally make public
  • Respond to feedback and support requests
  • Diagnose bugs and technical problems
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service
  • Improve Daily Inspo's features, usability, and performance
  • Enforce our Terms of Use
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Local Storage and Similar Technologies

Daily Inspo may use browser storage technologies such as local storage, IndexedDB, or similar mechanisms to provide features including:

  • Offline access
  • Quote storage and caching
  • User preferences
  • Remembering whether onboarding has been completed
  • Maintaining application state

These technologies are an important part of Daily Inspo's offline-first functionality.

If Daily Inspo later introduces cookies, analytics, advertising, or additional tracking technologies, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

4. Service Providers

We may rely on third-party service providers to operate Daily Inspo. These providers may include companies that provide:

  • Database infrastructure
  • Authentication
  • Hosting
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Email delivery
  • Security and technical infrastructure

For example, Daily Inspo currently uses Supabase for portions of its backend infrastructure. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to Daily Inspo and subject to their own privacy and security practices.

5. Public Content

If Daily Inspo allows you to designate a quote, profile, article, or other content as public, information you intentionally make public may be viewable by other users and visitors.

You should not publish information that you do not want others to see. Changing content from public to private may prevent future public display, but copies, screenshots, cached versions, or information previously shared by others may continue to exist outside Daily Inspo's control.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information and user content only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate Daily Inspo, provide requested features, resolve disputes, maintain security, or satisfy legal obligations.

Daily Inspo may use soft-deletion or recovery periods for certain content before permanent deletion. Locally stored data may remain on your browser or device until it is deleted by the application, removed by you, or cleared through your browser or device settings.

If you would like to request deletion of account information or cloud-stored personal data, contact support@daily-inspo.app.

As Daily Inspo develops additional account-management features, deletion controls may also become available directly inside the product.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled through Daily Inspo.

However, no online service, database, or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You use Daily Inspo with the understanding that no system can eliminate all security risk.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials and for notifying us if you believe your account has been compromised.

8. Your Choices

Depending on how you use Daily Inspo, you may be able to:

  • Use certain features without creating an account
  • Keep content locally on your device
  • Choose whether certain content is public or private
  • Edit or delete quotes
  • Request deletion of cloud-stored account information
  • Choose whether to provide an email address when submitting certain feedback

You may contact us at support@daily-inspo.app with questions or requests concerning your information.

9. Children's Privacy

Daily Inspo is not directed to children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that information from a child under 13 has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10. International Users

Daily Inspo is operated from the United States.

If you access Daily Inspo from another country, your information may be processed or stored in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate. Privacy protections may differ from those in your country of residence.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Daily Inspo may update this Privacy Policy as the product changes.

When material changes are made, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice when appropriate. You should review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about our practices.
